# Responsible use

TraceLens is intended for learning, evidence organization, and research on domains you own or are authorized to assess.

## Working with observations

- Keep the source and collection time with each observation.
- Review the context before drawing conclusions.
- Re-check important information independently.
- Avoid saving personal or confidential information in project reports.
- Share reports only with people who are authorized to receive them.

## Understanding results

Domain information can change over time and may differ between providers. A missing item in one report is not proof that it does not exist. Treat each result as a time-stamped observation rather than a definitive conclusion.
